Terms, Privacy and Safety

Last updated November 8, 2026

Using Mythic Spell Swap

  • Mythic Spell Swap lets players list Magic: The Gathering cards, view card prices, message each other and find traders nearby.
  • You must be 13 or older to make an account.
  • Keep listings honest: real cards, accurate condition and fair descriptions. No counterfeits or proxies sold as real.
  • No harassment, spam, scams or hateful content. Accounts that break these rules may be removed.

Payments and trades

  • Mythic Spell Swap does not process payments or hold money. Buyers and sellers arrange payment and shipping themselves.
  • Mythic Spell Swap is not responsible for trades between users. Trade at your own risk.

Staying safe

  • Meet in public places, like a local game store, for in-person trades.
  • Use payment methods with buyer protection. Never pay with gift cards.
  • Check card condition before you pay, and be careful with deals that seem too good to be true.

Privacy

  • We store your email, username, optional city, profile picture, listings and messages so the site works.
  • Your email and password are never shown to other users.
  • Messages can only be read by you and the person you are talking to.
  • The trader map is opt-in. If you turn it on, only your approximate area (about 3 miles off) is shown, never your exact location. You can turn it off anytime in your profile.
  • We do not sell your information.

Card prices

Prices come from Scryfall (TCGplayer market data) and may not match what a card actually sells for.
